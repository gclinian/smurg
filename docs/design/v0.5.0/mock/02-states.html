smurg v0.5.0: states of the conversation column

A specification sheet, not a screen: every state of the cards, the composer and the session header that the screens do not show at once. Each cell is the real markup of the mock at the width of a 3-column layout. The roles are those of smurg: Host (Ian), Agent access (Mei, Ken), Editor (Amy), Viewer (Leo).
